## 040000 Opening
Deep in the mountains lived a happy family of three.

But one morning, little Kyoko fell ill with a high fever. Her worried mother remembered the legend of the River King, and told her son:

"Find the River King, and be sure to catch it!"

So Taro took his fishing rod and set off for the river.
## 0A0800 Next morning
When Taro woke up, he shouted at the top of his voice: "Today I'm going to catch the River King for sure!"

His sister begged him with tears in her eyes: "Really? This time you have to catch it, okay?" His mother begged him too.

So Taro set off to fish the river once again.
## 037800 Came home without the River King

"I'm looking after her, so don't worry. Hurry and catch the River King!"

## 087800 Day ended without the River King

His mother was disappointed, and said to him: "Tomorrow, be sure to catch the River King!"

## 040800 Ending
When she saw the River King, their mother was overjoyed. Since long ago, it had been said that catching the River King would cure any illness.

Just as the legend said, little Kyoko got better before their eyes. Grateful, their mother carefully returned the River King to the river the next day.
