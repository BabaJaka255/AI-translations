# Spot sign text, English. One block per spot: "## N Name", then 5 lines (one per bullet line of the Japanese).
# Each line wraps at 21 columns onto at most 2 rows. Line 5 has no bullet, so it reads as part of line 4.
## 1 Char (Iwana)
Lives in cold, high mountain streams.
Grows to 30-80cm.
Use an insect-like fly, sunk underwater.
Bob the fly up and down to draw it in.
It comes closest as the fly is lowered.
## 2 Masu Trout
Lives in streams just downstream of char.
Grows to 30-50cm.
Float an insect-like fly on the surface.
While the fly drifts in the current,
a masu trout will rise and snap at it.
## 3 Rainbow Trout
Lives in cold, clear streams.
Grows to 40-60cm.
Sink the fly and let it drift.
Lift the fly up near the surface, and
the trout will eagerly give chase.
## 4 White Crucian
Lives in mountain lakes and the like.
Grows to 40-60cm.
Use paste bait; wait at the right depth.
Wait until the bait has worn small,
and it will bite. Strike quickly!
## 5 Black Bass
Lives in lakes and the like.
Grows to about 50cm.
Tempt it with a fish-shaped lure.
The lure sinks, so reel it up, then
let it sink again to lure the bass.
## 6 Dace
Lives all along the river, top to bottom.
Grows to 30-40cm.
Drift a maggot under a ball float.
Bob the bait gently just off the bottom
and the dace will come close.
## 7 Pale Chub
Lives where the river flows gently.
Grows to about 13cm.
Drift a maggot under a ball float.
Bob the bait a little up off the bottom
and a pale chub will swim up.
## 8 Crucian Carp
Lives where the river flows gently.
Grows to about 30cm.
Float-fish with a red worm.
Lift the bait and the carp will follow,
but lift it too far and it swims off.
## 9 Catfish
Lives on muddy beds of slow rivers.
Grows to 30-60cm.
Tempt it with a noisy lure.
When a catfish shows up, pull the lure
from the bottom to the top in one go!
## 10 Carp
Lives on muddy beds of slow rivers.
Grows to 50-60cm.
Cast out a worm and let it settle.
Lift the bait; once it sinks to the
right depth, the carp swims up to eat.
## 11 River Prawn
Lives where the water flows gently.
Grows to 12-13cm.
Bait a red worm; sinker on the bottom.
Lift the bait and let it sink. Repeat,
and the prawn will pounce on the bait.
## 12 Eel
Lives on sandy or muddy bottoms.
Grows to 40-90cm.
Cast out a worm and let it settle.
Lift the bait; once it sinks to the
right spot, the eel comes to eat it.
## 13 Bitterling
Lives in ponds and the like.
Grows to 7-10cm.
Float-fish with a bloodworm.
It pecks at the bait again and again;
strike only when it takes it in!
## 14 Stone Moroko
Lives on the muddy bottoms of ponds.
Grows to about 10cm.
Float-fish with a bloodworm.
It darts about in front of the bait,
but sooner or later it will eat it.
## 15 River King
Lives in lowland marshes and the like.
Grows to about 90cm.
Tempt it with a floating lure.
Once it has come right up to the lure,
wait for it to rise near the surface.
