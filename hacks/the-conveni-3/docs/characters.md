# Characters and voice

Status: approved 2026-11-02.
Honorifics: kept (contemporary policy, see translation-style-policy). Line width is about 16 full-width
characters, so every voice has to stay short.

## Angel-san A, the drawler (えんぜるさん)
- Role: tutorial / tips angel (scenario hints, store layout, fixtures).
- JP: soft, slow, stretched; every phrase ends in ～ and lines end in ～ですの～ / ～ますの～.
- English voice: dreamy and polite, with the pauses kept as "~" ("Shelves hold~ more goods than carts~").
  Contractions OK. Not baby talk.
- Example: 棚は～ワゴンよりも～たくさん商品を置くことができますの～。
  → "Shelves~ hold more stock~ than carts do~."

## Angel-san B, the brisk advisor
- Role: money, tax, staff advice.
- JP: confident older-sister feminine speech, ～わ / ～わよ / ～なさい!
- English voice: crisp, a little bossy, warm underneath. "Don't be stingy, or your clerks will quit!"
- Example: ケチケチしてると店員がいなくなっちゃうこともあるから気をつけなさい！
  → "Pinch pennies and your clerks walk out. Careful!"

## Angel-san C, the cheerful guide
- Role: main tutorial walk-through, goals.
- JP: polite です／ます with ♪ and ～ね.
- English voice: friendly coach, keeps the ♪ (the font has it).
- Example: ２号店を作りましょう。メニューの「新規出店」でできますよ♪
  → "Let's open Store #2! Use Open New Store.♪"

UNSURE: whether A, B and C are separate angels or one angel with moods; the art should tell. Checking in the emulator.

## System text
- Neutral, short, title case for menu items and goals ("Reach ¥10 Billion in Total Sales").
- Goal prompts written as commands (～せよ / ～を競え) become imperatives: "Sell 1,000 Karaage-kun!"
