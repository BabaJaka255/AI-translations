# Characters and voice: Hamster Club

Approved by the project lead on 2026-10-30.

## Setting and honorific policy

Contemporary Japan: a kid raising hamsters, a neighborhood pet shop, an allowance
from their parents, a rich girl from a fancy house. Per the style guide we
**keep honorifics** (-san, -kun, -chan).

The game makes this unusually visible: the shop owner asks how to address the
player and offers さん / クン / ちゃん / よびすて (no honorific). That menu stays
as " -san / -kun / -chan / None", and the chosen suffix is glued onto the
player's name in her lines ("<name>-chan"). Hamster names that contain honorifics
keep them too (Pu-chan, Endo-kun, Oscar-kun).

The player's gender is never stated. The honorific choice is the player's own
way of hinting at it, so English lines never add he/she for the player.

## Shop owner (ショップのおねえさん)
- Role: runs the Hamster Club pet shop. Tutorial voice, sells everything, treats
  sick hamsters, comforts you when one dies. Faces 00 (normal), 0B (sad).
- Japanese: friendly, chatty, feminine casual speech: わ, よ, ね, かしら,
  ちょーだい. Switches to shop politeness for set phrases (いらっしゃいませ,
  ありがとうございました, ですよ when warning you).
- English voice: warm big-sister shopkeeper. Contractions, light exclamation,
  "hon"-free (no Americanized diner speech). Questions soften with "maybe?",
  "how about...?". Set phrases stay short and polite: "Welcome!", "Thank you,
  come again!"
- Honorifics: calls the player `<name><honorific>` as chosen. Calls hamsters
  このコ = "this little one".
- Examples:
  - いらっしゃいませ。ハムスターくらぶへようこそ！ → "Welcome! Welcome to the Hamster Club!" (or "Hi there! Welcome to the Hamster Club!")
  - あら？はじめてみるおきゃくさんね。 → "Oh? I don't think we've met before."
  - よければ、なまえをおしえてくれるかしら？ → "Would you tell me your name?"
  - エサはぜったいきらしたらダメよ。 → "Never, ever let the food run out, okay?"
  - げんきだしてね！わかれもあるけど、あたらしいであいだってあるんだから。 → "Cheer up! There are goodbyes, but there are new friends too."

## Rival girl (お嬢様, unnamed)
- Role: rich rival with the champion hamster Josephine. Mocks your "commoner
  hamster", gifts Oscar-kun, challenges you by letter, final Special Cup
  opponent. Faces 01-04 (02 = laughing pose).
- Japanese: textbook ojou-sama: ワタクシ, ですわ / ますわ / ですの,
  ごきげんよう, さしあげますわ, the お〜ほっほっほ laugh, クスッ (giggle).
  Calls the player `<name>っち`, a cutesy-condescending nickname she invents
  from the name you give her (you can give her a fake one).
- English voice: grand and prim, a little theatrical. Full forms and fancy words
  ("I shall", "my dear", "how utterly common"), almost no contractions, a
  sprinkle of "darling". Not a British accent, just high register. She never
  swears; her insults are snobbery ("commoner hamster").
- Catchphrases: "Oh-ho-ho-ho-ho-ho!" (always the full laugh), "Good day!" for
  ごきげんよう. Nickname: `<name>-chi`.
- Examples:
  - そんな、しょみんハムスターなんて、いくらそだてても、かわいくなりませんわよ。 → "Oh, please. No matter how much you raise that commoner hamster, it will never be cute."
  - ふ〜ん、<name>なの。さえないですわね・・・。 → "Hmm, <name>, is it? How dreadfully plain..."
  - <name>っち・・・なんてすてきなひびきかしら。 → "<name>-chi... What a lovely ring it has."
  - では、ごきげんよう、お〜ほっほっほっほっほっほっ！ → "Well then, good day! Oh-ho-ho-ho-ho-ho!"

## Rival boy (unnamed)
- Role: quiet, serious breeder you bump into at the HAM'S Cup. Explains the
  Breeders' Cup and gives blunt care advice. Faces 05 (normal), 06 (flustered).
- Japanese: terse, masculine plain form: だ, だな, だぞ, おまえ, long silences
  (・・・・・・), フッ. Catchphrase また、あおう。 Gets flustered when the player
  picks the joke option すきだっ！ ("I love you!"): な！？…かえる・・・。
- English voice: few words, short sentences, contractions fine, cool but not
  rude. Silences stay as "......". フッ = "Heh." Advice lines are plain and
  direct, like an older kid who knows his stuff.
- Catchphrase: "We'll meet again." (every time, word for word).
- Examples:
  - みかけないカオだな。HAM'Sカップは、はじめてか？ → "Haven't seen you around. First HAM'S Cup?"
  - あそびのつもりなら、やめておけ。 → "If you're just playing around, don't bother."
  - な！？ ・・・・・・。かえる・・・。 → "Wh-what!? ...... I'm going home..."
  - もっとハムスターとあそんでやれ。 → "Play with your hamster more."

## Player (silent)
- Only speaks through menu choices, which are short and casual:
  ムカツクね〜ちゃん → "What a snob!" (8-character menu limit applies),
  すきだっ！ → "I love you!" The option sits alone in its row, so it has 17 columns and keeps "I love you!".

## Narration and system text
- Faces 07 (lost hamster search), 08 (babies), 09 (letter), plus allowance and
  care results. Japanese is neutral です/ます. English: plain, clear, neutral,
  present tense for results ("Your hamster is gone. Let's look elsewhere.").

## Contest announcers (outside the event script, dumped later)
- Charm contest host: polite TV host ("Hello, everyone!!").
- Pretty Cup host: hyper, stretched vowels (こーんばーんはーー！！) → "Good eeevening!!"; keep the stretch, it's the character.
- Leapfrog contest host: sports commentator, hushed suspense.
