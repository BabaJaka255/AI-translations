River King Glossary川のぬし釣り２ 用語集

Every fish, creature, item, place, character and game term in Super Legend of the River King, next to the original Japanese. Use it to keep names consistent across other fishing-game translations.

The River King

ぬし is always River King, after the Game Boy Legend of the River King games.

Fish names

Common English names. Where none exists, the Japanese name is kept: Itomoroko, Mugitsuku, Tamoroko, Wataka, Higai.

River and sea-run forms

River forms are Trout, sea-run forms Salmon: ヤマメ Masu Trout / サクラマス Masu Salmon; アマゴ Redspot Trout / サツキマス Redspot Salmon.

Short forms

Map labels fit 9 characters and the fishery window 13. ヘラブナ becomes W.Crucian wherever space is tight.

Gear

サオ Rod, ウキ Float, オモリ Sinker, ハリ Hook. The fishing styles are Float, Casting, Lure and Fly.

Money and counters

Money is shown in yen. Japanese counters such as 尾, 匹 and 才 are dropped.

The Japanese was read from the game's own font and checked by hand. Misread kanji were corrected (for example 昆虫, 型, 産卵). One cooking note is marked as unverified.

Romaji is Hepburn. Macrons appear only for katakana long marks (ー) and hand-written readings. Scientific names are included for reference only and don't appear in the game.
