440|If you want stone flounder, use a Surf Rod and a Flounder Hook.{D}For bait, sandworms or shellfish work well.
441|If you want rice octopus, use a Reef Rod{D}with a Crab Octo Rig. That'll do the trick.
442|To catch tongue sole, use a Surf Rod.{D}Ragworms or small abalone make good bait.
443|Wear leather gloves when you go after rabbitfish.{D}Use a Reef Rod, with ragworms or laver for bait.
444|To catch cuttlefish,{D}put a Dropper Rig on a Boat Fast rod.{D}Spiny lobster or jack mackerel make good bait.{D}I hear they sell for a lot.
445|To catch a sunfish, use a Boat Big rod.{D}Use krill or fish strips for bait.
446|If you want bluefin tuna, go trolling.{D}The rod, of course, is the X-heavy 130lb.{D}Use the Flying Fish decoy hook.
447|If you want a blue marlin,{D}go trolling with the X-heavy 130lb rod.{D}Use the Kagura Marlin decoy hook.
448|For kelp grouper, use a Grouper Rod.{D}The hook is the Grouper Hook, of course.{D}For bait, try fish or squid, still alive.
449|For sea bass,{D}use a Reef Rod and pick a sturdy hook.{D}Ragworms or sandworms make good bait.{D}They bite well on lures, too.{D}Try some night fishing for them, too.
450|For scorpionfish,{D}use a Reef Rod with a Kaizu Hook.{D}For bait, try ragworms, pileworms or krill.{D}Wear leather gloves so you don't get hurt.
451|For flying squid, use a Boat Squid rod.{D}With a Squid Sutte Rig you can catch them without bait.
452|For opaleye,{D}put a Standing Float on a Reef Rod and use an Opaleye Hook.{D}Shrimp or green laver make good bait.
453|To catch halfbeak,{D}put a float on a Light Rod and use a Halfbeak Hook.{D}For bait, try grass shrimp or krill.
454|For flounder,{D}use a Boat Live rod with a sinker and a Dropper Rig.{D}Jack mackerel or mackerel should make good bait.{D}They sell for a lot.

457|To catch red sea bream,{D}use a boat rod with an Arm Sinker and a Sea Bream Hook.{D}Use grass shrimp or krill for bait.{D}They sell for a lot.
458|To catch goby,{D}put a float on a Light Rod and use a Goby Hook.{D}Ragworms or shellfish make good bait.{D}You can also catch them casting with a Surf Rod.
459|For croaker,{D}use a Surf Rod. Ragworms or sandworms for bait, I'd say.
460|For whiting,{D}go casting with a Surf Rod.{D}For bait, ragworms, sandworms and pileworms are all good.
