# Characters and voice (Umi no Nushi Tsuri PS1)

**Honorific policy:** contemporary rural Japan, so name suffixes are kept as spoken (Yuko-san, Hachi-san,
{name}-san from officials, Dolphin-san is rendered "Mr. Dolphin"). Kinship words are translated: お兄ちゃん "Brother"
(Sachiko to Koichi), じいちゃん/おじいさん Grandpa, ばあちゃん/おばあさん Grandma. Generic おじさん "mister".
ご隠居(様) is "the Old Master" (a title, as in English Mito Komon subtitles).

Playable (names editable; {7:n} tokens):
- **Koichi** 光一 ({7:1}), 12, elementary-school boy. Brave, impulsive, casual ("I'll catch it, you'll see!").
- **Sachiko** 幸子 ({7:2}), his sick little sister; later playable. Polite, gentle, firm when it counts.
- **Oyaji** おやじ ({7:3}), tackle-shop owner who only wants to go fishing. Gruff, "Ha!" Name kept as the default
  name "Pops".  UNSURE: default name choice.
- **Kenichi** 健一 ({7:4}), his shy son, in love with Yuko. Stammers ("Th-that's...").

Others:
- Grandpa (archaic old-man speech: "I say", "Hmph"), Grandma (kind; secretly packs bento every time).
- Cat ネコ: ends lines with "meow" (にゃ).
- Hachi ハチ, Yashichi ヤシチ, Suke スケ, Kaku カク, Ogin お銀 (bath gag) and the Old Master ご隠居:
  a Mito Komon parody (retired lord traveling incognito with companions, the inro). Speed-fishing rivals talk tough.
- The Leader リーダー: kid gang boss who talks like a movie tough guy; loves chocolate.
- Tournament Official 大会役員: polite. Boatman 船頭, Fisherman 漁師, Angler 釣り師, Lookout 見張り, Sailor 船員.
- Yuko 優子: shop girl glued to the TV; teases Kenichi. Noriko 紀子 seeks a youth-restoring fish.
  Taro 太郎 (looking for Sanpei 三平). Young Man 若者 who met the Sea King. Dolphin イルカ.
