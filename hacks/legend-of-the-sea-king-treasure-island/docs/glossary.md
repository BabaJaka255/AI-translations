# Umi no Nushi Tsuri: Takarajima ni Mukatte (PS1) - glossary

Follows the series glossary (series_glossary.md). Fish, bait, gear, food, item and creature names come
from our Super Legend of the Sea King (SFC) patch (`../../super-legend-of-the-sea-king/src/script/`): same developer, same data.
